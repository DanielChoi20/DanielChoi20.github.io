Teaching

Helping students think like managers, not just solve like analysts.

I create active classrooms where students connect analytical tools to operating decisions, test ideas, and learn to ask better questions.

Teaching philosophy

Three principles

01

Earn attention

Simulations, current business examples, and industry voices make operational ideas tangible and worth engaging with.

02

Adapt deliberately

I respond to students' needs and to changing learning environments while keeping the intellectual bar high.

03

Develop judgment

Students practice defining the right problem, understanding the system, finding root causes, and evaluating risk.

In the classroom

Learning operations by operating

I use the Beer Game, Newsvendor Game, and an AI-augmented Beer Game alongside a semester-long entrepreneurship project. Students prototype ideas, run customer experiments, and connect concepts such as process analysis, inventory, coordination, and capacity to real decisions.

Generative AI can accelerate analysis, but managers still need to frame the problem, understand causal structure, and judge whether a proposed solution is robust. My assignments are designed to strengthen precisely those skills.
