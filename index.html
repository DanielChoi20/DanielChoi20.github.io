Research focus

From ambitious targets to workable systems.

My research combines empirical methods and analytical modeling to understand corporate climate commitments, transition risk, recycled-content policy, and the design of circular supply chains.

About

Research grounded in practice

I am advised by André Calmon and Beril Toktay. My dissertation, Essays on Corporate Sustainability Targets, develops an agenda around firms' transition to low-carbon operations and circular business models.

Before Georgia Tech, I was a researcher at KAIST's Center for Sustainable Development and an instructor officer at the Korea Army Academy. I enjoy collaborating with companies and industry coalitions to develop practice-grounded research questions.
